\# Brand & Persona Guidelines

\#\# Core Brand Identity  
\*   \*\*Brand Name/Domain:\*\* Michael Wolfenberger Templates (michaelwolfenberger.com)  
\*   \*\*Vibe:\*\* Engineered, logical, minimalist, and unbreakable.   
\*   \*\*Core Promise:\*\* "Systems built with computer science rigor, designed for everyday utility."

\#\# Target Personas  
1\.  \*\*The Analytical Optimizer:\*\* Wants to track every penny, minute, or calorie. Appreciates robust formulas and hates when templates break under edge cases.  
2\.  \*\*The Overwhelmed Professional:\*\* Needs a system that works out of the box. Wants clean dashboards without distracting clutter.  
3\.  \*\*The Technical Creator:\*\* Values clean database schemas, proper naming conventions, and modular design.

\#\# Tone & Voice Guardrails  
\*   \*\*DO:\*\* Write with authority, precision, and clarity. Use active voice.   
\*   \*\*DO:\*\* Explain \*why\* a system is built a certain way if it adds value (e.g., "This uses a zero-based allocation model to ensure...").  
\*   \*\*DO NOT:\*\* Use corporate jargon or fluffy marketing speak.   
\*   \*\*BANNED WORDS:\*\* delve, unlock, unleash, seamless, holistic, synergy, journey, elevate.
