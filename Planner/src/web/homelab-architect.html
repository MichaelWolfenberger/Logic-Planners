What's Inside

Six tabs. Every angle of your infrastructure covered.

Each tab is a self-contained module. Use all six together or pull out the ones your build needs.

🌐

Network Topology Planner

Map your physical and logical network in a structured table. Document subnets, VLAN IDs, tagged ports, and device-to-switch assignments. Includes a DNS record log and firewall rule tracker.

🐳

Service & Container Inventory

Track every Docker container, VM, and bare-metal service: host machine, image tag, exposed ports, mount points, restart policy, and last-updated date. Status column uses conditional formatting to flag stale or stopped services.

💽

Storage Allocation Matrix

Per-drive breakdown with model, capacity, interface type, RAID membership, current usage, and projected fill date. Calculates available headroom across your entire storage pool with automatic totals.

⚡

Power Consumption Calculator

Input idle and load wattages per device. The workbook calculates total draw, monthly kWh estimate, and UPS load percentage. Flags configurations that exceed your UPS capacity before it becomes a problem.

🛒

Hardware Procurement Tracker

Log planned and purchased components with price, vendor, purchase date, and warranty expiry. Running total shows cumulative build cost so you always know the true number, not just what you remember spending.

🔧

Maintenance Log

Record completed tasks with date, affected system, and resolution notes. A scheduling column calculates next-due dates for recurring tasks (certificate renewal, drive health checks, OS updates) based on your defined intervals.
