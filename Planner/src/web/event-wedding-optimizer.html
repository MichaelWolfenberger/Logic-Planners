What's Inside

Six tabs. From initial budget to day-of execution.

Each module is independent. Use all six or pull out the ones your event requires.

💰

Budget vs. Actual Tracker

Set a total event budget and allocate it across categories (venue, catering, photography, florals, etc.). Log committed and actual costs as they occur. Variance columns flag each category as over or under in real time.

🏢

Vendor Comparison Matrix

Compare up to 3 vendors per category with columns for quoted price, deposit amount, deposit due date, cancellation policy, contract signed status, and contact information. Includes a recommended-vendor selection column.

👥

Guest List Manager

Track every guest with RSVP status, meal selection, dietary restrictions, plus-one status, and table assignment. Summary row shows confirmed headcount, pending, and declined — feeding directly into the per-head cost calculator.

🕐

Day-Of Timeline Builder

Build a minute-by-minute run-of-show with start time, duration, location, responsible party, and notes per item. Buffer time between events is calculated automatically to catch scheduling conflicts before the day itself.

📅

Payment Milestone Tracker

A master list of every payment due: vendor, amount, due date, payment method, and status. Conditional formatting flags overdue and upcoming payments. Totals show how much has been paid vs. what's still outstanding.

🧮

Per-Head Cost Calculator

Enter your confirmed guest count and the calculator derives cost-per-head for every budget category plus a total. A scenario model lets you test the cost impact of different attendance numbers before finalizing vendor minimums.
