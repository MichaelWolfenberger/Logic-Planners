How do I get the files after purchase?
Immediately after payment, Gumroad sends a download link to the email address you used at checkout. Click the link, download the .xlsx file, and open it in Google Sheets or Excel. No account creation on our end is required — it is a direct file download with no expiry.
Do I need a paid Google or Microsoft account?
No. All templates are standard .xlsx files. A free Google account (Google Sheets) is sufficient. Microsoft Excel 2016 or any version of Microsoft 365 also works. No paid tiers, no add-ons, and no third-party service access is required.
What format are the templates delivered in?
All templates are delivered as .xlsx files. This format is compatible with Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel for Windows, Microsoft Excel for Mac (2016+), and LibreOffice Calc 7.x. Every formula uses standard functions — no proprietary add-ins or macros are required.
Will the formulas work correctly on Excel for Mac?
Yes. All templates are tested against Microsoft 365 (Mac and Windows) and Google Sheets. Array formulas use standard syntax for cross-platform compatibility. If you encounter any formula error after opening the file, email support@logicplanners.com with a screenshot and it will be diagnosed and corrected.
Can I customize the categories and variables?
Yes — that is the intended workflow. Every template includes a dedicated Setup tab where you configure all global variables: category names, income sources, tax rates, thresholds, and more. Input cells are clearly marked with a distinct background color. Formula cells are locked to prevent accidental changes. You configure the system through the Setup tab; you do not touch any formula cells directly.
What is your refund policy?
7-day full refund, no questions asked. If the template does not do what the product description states it does, email support@logicplanners.com within 7 days of purchase and a full refund will be issued through Gumroad. Refunds typically appear within 3–5 business days depending on your bank.
Can I share the template with a partner or co-planner?
One license covers one user. Sharing with a spouse, partner, or co-planner working on the same project you purchased the template for is permitted. Redistribution, resale, or sharing with unrelated individuals or organizations is not permitted under the standard license.
Will templates be updated after I buy?
Yes. Gumroad sends updated file versions to your original download link automatically. When a template receives a significant update, existing customers get access to the new version at no additional cost. Bug fixes are always free.
