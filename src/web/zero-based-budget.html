The Zero-Based Method

Income minus allocations equals zero.

Most budgets track spending after the fact. Zero-based budgeting forces allocation before any spending occurs. Every dollar is assigned a category — savings, fixed costs, variable spend, debt — until the remaining balance is exactly $0.

This isn't about restriction. It's about intention. When you know where every dollar goes before the month begins, overspending in one category is a conscious trade-off, not a surprise.

The workbook enforces this model structurally. The allocation tab will not clear to $0 until your categories fully account for your income. The variance tracker then shows exactly where reality diverged from the plan.
