How to Use

From download to your first vendor meeting in 10 minutes.

All wedding templates are ready to use immediately. Here's the exact setup process.

1
Purchase & Download

Buy on Etsy or Gumroad. Your .xlsx file downloads instantly. Save it to Google Drive so you can access it anywhere — including from your phone during vendor meetings.

2
Fill the Setup Tab

Enter your wedding date, total budget, guest count estimate, and vendor categories. These values propagate through every tab automatically. Takes 5 minutes.

3
Log Vendors & Guests

As you collect quotes, add them to the vendor matrix. As RSVPs come in, log them in the guest list. The budget tracker and per-head calculator update in real time.

4
Track Through the Big Day

Use the payment tracker for deposit deadlines. Build the day-of timeline 2–4 weeks out. Everything lives in one file — share a view-only link with your coordinator, planner, or partner.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I share these with my wedding planner or coordinator?

Yes. In Google Sheets, use File → Share → "Anyone with the link can view" to send a read-only link. Your coordinator sees the data but can't edit it unless you grant edit access.

What if I need more vendor categories than the default?

All vendor categories are defined in the Setup tab. Add new categories there and they appear as dropdown options in the vendor matrix and budget tracker automatically.

Do I need the Complete Optimizer if I already bought the Guest List Manager?

The Complete Optimizer includes a guest management tab, but it's less detailed than the standalone Guest List Manager. If seating logistics are complex (100+ guests, multiple meal options), get both.

Is the Honeymoon Planner designed for international trips?

Yes. The Setup tab includes a currency field. All cost columns display in your chosen currency. A foreign exchange rate field lets you track spending in local currency alongside your home currency.
