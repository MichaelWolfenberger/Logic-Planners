FAQ

Common questions.

Do the formulas work if I have irregular income?

Yes. The Setup tab has a field for irregular income where you enter your lowest expected monthly take-home. Budgeting from your floor — not your ceiling — is best practice for variable earners. When a higher month comes in, you log the extra as a category called "surplus" or roll it into savings.

Can I use the debt tracker for more than 10 debts?

The default supports 15 debts. To add more, insert rows in the debt table — the payoff calculator and payment schedule are structured to auto-detect new rows. No formulas to update manually.

Does the annual dashboard connect to the monthly budget?

They're separate files by design — keeping them independent means neither breaks when you update the other. The annual dashboard has a manual input column for monthly totals you copy over from the budget tracker. Takes about two minutes per month.

What if my budget categories don't match the defaults?

All budget categories are defined in the Setup tab as a simple list. Change them there and every dropdown in the expense log updates automatically. The defaults (housing, food, transport, etc.) are just starting points — entirely replaceable.
