How to Use

From purchase to your first planning session in 15 minutes.

All event planner templates are pre-structured. You're entering your event details, not building the system.

1
Purchase & Download

Buy on Etsy or Gumroad. Your .xlsx file is available immediately. Save it to Google Drive for easy sharing with co-planners or vendors.

2
Fill the Setup Tab

Enter the event name, date, expected headcount, total budget, and vendor categories. These populate across every tab automatically.

3
Build Out Each Tab

Add vendors, speakers, or guests as you confirm them. The budget tracker updates in real time. Payment deadlines are flagged automatically as they approach.

4
Execute & Reconcile

Use the run-of-show on the day itself. After the event, use the reconciliation tab to compare planned vs. actual spend — useful for budgeting the next one.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I reuse the templates for recurring events?

Yes. Duplicate the workbook each time. Budget categories, vendor lists, and task phases carry over, and all formulas stay intact — a quarterly off-site takes about 5 minutes to set up from the prior copy.

How many attendees can the Guest & Registration Manager handle?

The attendee list ships with 500 rows, and the dashboard counts update automatically as you add registrations. Adding more rows is simple duplication — it's been tested past 500 rows in Google Sheets without performance issues.

Do the templates work for any event type?

Yes. Budget categories, ticket types, task phases, and vendor service categories are all editable text — rename them to fit a conference, fundraiser, birthday, gala, or corporate off-site. The formulas don't care what you call things.

Do the four templates work together?

Each one is self-contained, so you can use one or all four. Together they cover the four systems every event needs: money (Budget Tracker), people (Registration Manager), time (Project Timeline), and suppliers (Vendor & Logistics Manager).
