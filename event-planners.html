How to Use

From purchase to your first planning session in 15 minutes.

All event planner templates are pre-structured. You're entering your event details, not building the system.

1
Purchase & Download

Buy on Etsy or Gumroad. Your .xlsx file is available immediately. Save it to Google Drive for easy sharing with co-planners or vendors.

2
Fill the Setup Tab

Enter the event name, date, expected headcount, total budget, and vendor categories. These populate across every tab automatically.

3
Build Out Each Tab

Add vendors, speakers, or guests as you confirm them. The budget tracker updates in real time. Payment deadlines are flagged automatically as they approach.

4
Execute & Reconcile

Use the run-of-show on the day itself. After the event, use the reconciliation tab to compare planned vs. actual spend — useful for budgeting the next one.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I use the Corporate Event Planner for recurring events?

Yes. Duplicate the entire workbook each time. The Setup tab lets you change event name, date, and budget without touching any formulas. Recurring events like quarterly off-sites take 5 minutes to set up from the prior copy.

How many attendees can the Birthday Organizer handle?

The guest list tab defaults to 200 rows. Adding more is a simple row duplication — formulas extend automatically. It's been tested to 500 rows without performance issues in Google Sheets.

Is the Dinner Party Planner usable for events with complex dietary requirements?

Yes. The dietary matrix supports up to 12 restriction types per guest (gluten-free, vegan, kosher, halal, nut allergy, etc.) and cross-references them against each menu item to flag conflicts before you shop.

Does the Conference Manager support multiple tracks or breakout sessions?

Yes. The agenda builder includes a room/track column and a parallel-session flag. Sessions scheduled at the same time in the same room are highlighted automatically, so scheduling conflicts are caught before you publish.
